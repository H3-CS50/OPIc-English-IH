# 5. Công viên & đi dạo (Going to Parks / Walking)

---

## Câu 1 – Mô tả công viên yêu thích

> **Q:** *"You indicated that you go to parks. Describe your favorite park. Where is it and what does it look like?"*

> My favorite park is Thong Nhat Park, which is right in the center of Hanoi, about ten minutes from my house by motorbike.
>
> It's a really big park with a large lake in the middle. There's a walking path all the way around the lake, and both sides of the path are lined with tall, old trees, so it's shady and cool even in summer.
>
> In one corner of the park, there's a playground for kids, and in another area there's some outdoor exercise equipment that anyone can use for free. You can also see lots of benches along the lake where people sit and chat or just watch the water.
>
> What I like most about this park is the atmosphere early in the morning. You see elderly people doing tai chi, groups of women dancing to music, and young people jogging. It feels so lively and peaceful at the same time.
>
> It's like a green escape from the noise and traffic of the city, and that's why I love going there.

## Câu 2 – Thường làm gì ở công viên

> **Q:** *"What do you usually do when you go to the park? Who do you go with?"*

> I usually go to the park three or four times a week, mostly in the early morning or after work.
>
> On weekday mornings, I go alone. I wake up at around five thirty, put on my running shoes, and jog around the lake two or three times. That takes about thirty minutes. After that, I do some stretching near the exercise area before going home to get ready for work.
>
> In the evening or on weekends, it's more relaxed. I often go with my roommate or my friends. We take a slow walk, chat about our day, and sometimes buy a drink from the street vendors near the gate. On Sunday afternoons, we sometimes bring a mat and some snacks and have a little picnic on the grass.
>
> Going to the park is the easiest way for me to clear my head. Even a short walk makes me feel much less stressed.

## Câu 3 – Kinh nghiệm đáng nhớ ở công viên

> **Q:** *"Tell me about a memorable or unexpected thing that happened to you at a park."*

> I have a heartwarming story about that. It happened last spring when I was walking in the park with my friend.
>
> We were walking along the lake, and suddenly we saw a little girl, maybe five years old, standing alone and crying. We asked her what was wrong, and she said she couldn't find her mom. She was really scared.
>
> So, we tried to calm her down. I bought her an ice cream, and my friend held her hand while we walked around looking for her mother. We asked people nearby, and we also went to the security office at the park gate to make an announcement.
>
> About twenty minutes later, a woman came running toward us, looking really worried. It was the girl's mom! She had been looking everywhere. She hugged her daughter and thanked us again and again.
>
> It was only a small thing, but I felt really good that we could help. Since then, whenever I go to the park, I notice how many little kids are running around, and I always keep an eye out for them.
