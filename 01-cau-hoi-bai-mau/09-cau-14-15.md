# 9. Câu 14–15: So sánh & vấn đề xã hội

Hai câu cuối là **câu khó nhất**, dùng để phân biệt IH và AL. Không cần hoàn hảo, nhưng **phải nói đủ dài và có lập luận**.

**Khung chung:**
1. Trả lời thẳng: *"I think … has changed a lot."*
2. **Quá khứ:** *"In the past / When I was young, people used to…"*
3. **Hiện tại:** *"Nowadays / These days, …"*
4. **Lý do** thay đổi: *"This is mainly because…"*
5. **Ưu – nhược điểm / ý kiến cá nhân**
6. Kết: *"So overall, …"*

## Câu 14 – So sánh

> **Q:** *"Talk about how the technology people use at home has changed over the years. What were things like in the past, and how are they different now?"*

> I think the technology we use at home has changed dramatically, even just in my lifetime.
>
> When I was a kid, in the early 2000s, my family only had a few electronic devices. We had one old TV with just a few channels, a landline phone, and a radio that my grandfather listened to every morning. If we wanted to talk to relatives far away, we had to make expensive phone calls, so we didn't do it very often.
>
> Nowadays, almost every home is full of smart devices. We have smart TVs, laptops, smartphones, and even things like robot vacuums and smart speakers that you can control with your voice. In my apartment, I can turn on the air conditioner from my phone before I get home, which is really convenient in summer.
>
> I think this change happened mainly because technology has become much cheaper and the internet is available everywhere.
>
> Of course, it has made our lives easier. But at the same time, I feel like family members talk to each other less. In the past, my whole family watched TV together in the evening. Now, everyone is looking at their own screen in their own room.
>
> So overall, technology has made home life more comfortable, but we need to make an effort to spend real time together.

## Câu 15 – Vấn đề xã hội / mối quan tâm

> **Q:** *"What are some concerns or issues people have about housing in your country? Why are these issues important?"*

> One of the biggest concerns people in Vietnam have about housing is that it's becoming too expensive, especially in big cities like Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.
>
> Over the last ten years, housing prices have gone up much faster than people's salaries. For example, a young couple with normal office jobs might have to save for twenty or thirty years to buy a small apartment. That's why many young people, including a lot of my friends, have to rent, or keep living with their parents even after they get married.
>
> Another issue is the quality of apartments. Some new buildings have problems like poor construction, not enough parking, or a lack of green space. Also, because so many people are moving to the cities, traffic and overcrowding are getting worse.
>
> I think these issues are important because having a stable home affects everything — people's mental health, their decision to start a family, and even the economy.
>
> In my opinion, the government should build more affordable housing for young people and develop better public transportation, so that people can live in the suburbs and still get to work easily.
>
> Personally, I'm still renting, but I hope that in five or ten years I'll be able to buy my own place.
