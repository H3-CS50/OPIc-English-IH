# 8. Role-play (Câu 11–13)

Role-play luôn đi theo bộ 3:

| Câu | Dạng | Bạn phải làm gì |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Hỏi thông tin** | Đặt **3–4 câu hỏi** cho "nhân viên/bạn bè" |
| 12 | **Giải quyết vấn đề** | Giải thích sự cố + đưa ra **2–3 phương án** |
| 13 | **Kinh nghiệm liên quan** | Kể một lần bạn gặp tình huống tương tự (thì quá khứ) |

> 🎭 **Quan trọng:** Hãy **diễn** như đang gọi điện thật. Mở đầu bằng lời chào, giới thiệu lý do gọi, kết thúc bằng lời cảm ơn. Giám khảo chấm cao những bài có tương tác tự nhiên.

## Bộ 1 – Phòng gym

### Câu 11 – Hỏi thông tin

> **Q:** *"You want to join a new gym in your neighborhood. Call the gym and ask three or four questions about it."*

> Hi, good morning. My name is Minh. I just moved into this neighborhood, and I saw your gym on my way home. I'm thinking about signing up, so I'd like to ask a few questions, if that's okay.
>
> First of all, what are your opening hours? I usually work until six, so I'd like to know if you're open late in the evening, and also on weekends.
>
> Second, how much is the membership? Do you have monthly or yearly plans? And is there any discount for new members or for students?
>
> Also, what kind of classes do you offer? I'm especially interested in yoga and maybe some boxing classes. Are they included in the membership, or do I have to pay extra?
>
> Oh, and one more thing — is there a parking area for motorbikes? Parking is a big problem around here.
>
> Okay, great. Thank you so much for the information. I think I'll come by this weekend to take a look. Have a nice day!

### Câu 12 – Giải quyết vấn đề

> **Q:** *"You signed up for the gym, but you've just found out that you have to go on a long business trip and can't use it for two months. Call the gym, explain the situation, and suggest two or three alternatives."*

> Hello, this is Minh. I signed up for a six-month membership at your gym about two weeks ago. I'm calling because I have a bit of a problem, and I hope you can help me.
>
> My company just told me that I have to go on a business trip abroad for two months, starting next week. So unfortunately, I won't be able to use the gym during that time, and I feel like it's a waste of money.
>
> I was wondering if we could work something out. I have a few ideas.
>
> First, would it be possible to freeze my membership for two months? Then I could continue using it when I come back.
>
> If that's not possible, maybe I could transfer the remaining months to my sister. She lives nearby and she's been wanting to join a gym anyway.
>
> Or, as a last option, could you give me a partial refund for the two months I can't use?
>
> I'd really appreciate it if you could let me know which option works best. Thank you so much for understanding.

### Câu 13 – Kinh nghiệm liên quan

> **Q:** *"Have you ever had a problem with something you signed up for or bought? Tell me what happened and how you solved it."*

> Yes, actually something similar happened to me last year. I bought a new pair of wireless earphones online during a big sale.
>
> When the package arrived, I was really excited, but when I opened it, I found out that the left earphone didn't work at all. I tried charging it and resetting it several times, but nothing worked.
>
> So I contacted the online store. At first, they didn't reply for two days, which was really frustrating. Then I sent them another message with a video showing the problem. After that, they finally answered and said they would send me a new pair, but I had to send the broken ones back first.
>
> I sent them back the next day, and about a week later, I received a new pair. Thankfully, this one worked perfectly.
>
> From that experience, I learned to always record a video when I open a package. It makes it much easier to prove that there's a problem.

## Bộ 2 – Đặt vé / khách sạn (ngắn gọn)

### Câu 11

> **Q:** *"You want to book a hotel room for your vacation. Call the hotel and ask three or four questions."*

> Hi, I'm calling to ask about booking a room for next month. I'm planning to stay for three nights, from the fifteenth to the eighteenth.
>
> First, do you have any rooms available on those dates? I'd prefer a double room with a sea view, if possible.
>
> How much is it per night? And does the price include breakfast?
>
> Also, how far is the hotel from the airport? Do you offer an airport pick-up service?
>
> And finally, what's your cancellation policy? In case my plans change, I'd like to know if I can cancel for free.
>
> Okay, that's very helpful. Thank you!

### Câu 12

> **Q:** *"When you arrive at the hotel, you find out that there's a problem with your reservation. Explain the situation to the receptionist and offer two or three solutions."*

> Excuse me, hi. I have a reservation under the name Nguyen Minh, but you said you can't find it in the system. That's strange, because I booked it online two weeks ago, and I received a confirmation email. Here, I can show it to you on my phone.
>
> I've been traveling all day, and I'm really tired, so I hope we can sort this out quickly.
>
> Could you check again with my email address or the booking number? Maybe there was a typo in my name.
>
> If the room I booked is not available, would it be possible to give me a similar room, or even upgrade me to a better one, since the mistake wasn't mine?
>
> If there are really no rooms left, could you please help me find another hotel nearby at a similar price, and cover the taxi fare to get there?
>
> Thank you, I really appreciate your help.

## Cụm từ role-play

| Tình huống | Cụm từ |
|---|---|
| Mở đầu | *Hi, I'm calling because… / I'd like to ask a few questions about…* |
| Hỏi lịch sự | *Could you tell me…? / I was wondering if… / Do you happen to know…?* |
| Chuyển câu hỏi | *First of all… / Also… / Oh, and one more thing…* |
| Nêu vấn đề | *I'm afraid there's a problem with… / I have a bit of a problem.* |
| Đưa phương án | *Would it be possible to…? / If that doesn't work, maybe we could… / As a last option…* |
| Thương lượng | *I was hoping we could work something out.* |
| Kết thúc | *Thank you so much for your help. / I really appreciate it. / Have a nice day!* |
