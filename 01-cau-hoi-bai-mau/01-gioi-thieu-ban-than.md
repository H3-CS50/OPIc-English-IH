# 1. Giới thiệu bản thân (Self-Introduction)

> **Câu hỏi:** *"Let's start the interview now. Tell me a little bit about yourself."*

Câu 1 thường không được tính điểm trực tiếp, nhưng giúp bạn **khởi động** và tạo ấn tượng đầu. Nói khoảng **1 phút – 1 phút 30**. Nên chuẩn bị kỹ vì đây là câu chắc chắn có.

**Khung:** Tên & tuổi → Đang làm gì (học/làm) → Sống ở đâu, với ai → Tính cách → Sở thích → Mục tiêu/kết.

---

## Bài mẫu 1 – Nhân viên văn phòng

> **Q:** *"Let's start the interview now. Tell me a little bit about yourself."*

> Hi, nice to meet you. My name is Minh, and I'm twenty-seven years old. I'm from Hanoi, and I've been living here my whole life, so I know the city pretty well.
>
> Right now, I'm working as an accountant at a logistics company. Honestly, the job can be a bit stressful, especially at the end of the month when we have to close the books, but I like it because I'm good with numbers and my coworkers are really friendly.
>
> I live in a small apartment with my younger sister. She's a university student, so we take turns cooking and cleaning, which works out pretty well.
>
> When it comes to my personality, I'd say I'm a calm and organized person. My friends often say I'm a good listener. In my free time, I usually watch movies, listen to music, or go for a walk in the park near my place. It really helps me recharge after a long day.
>
> These days, I'm studying English because my company works with a lot of foreign partners, and I want to communicate with them more confidently. So, that's a little bit about me.

## Bài mẫu 2 – Sinh viên

> **Q:** *"Let's start the interview now. Tell me a little bit about yourself."*

> Hello! My name is Lan, and I'm a third-year student at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My major is marketing, which I chose because I've always been interested in how brands connect with people.
>
> Actually, I'm originally from Da Nang, but I moved to Saigon for university, so now I share a dormitory room with two classmates. Living away from my family was hard at first, but I've gotten used to it, and I've become much more independent.
>
> I think I'm an outgoing and curious person. I love trying new things, like new cafés or new hobbies. On weekends, I usually hang out with my friends, go to the movies, or travel to nearby places when I have some extra money.
>
> I'm taking this test because I'm going to apply for an internship at an international company next year, and a good English score would really help. Anyway, that's me in a nutshell!

## Cụm từ đáng học

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa |
|---|---|
| I've been living here my whole life | Tôi sống ở đây cả đời |
| I'm good with numbers | Tôi giỏi tính toán |
| close the books | khoá sổ (kế toán) |
| take turns + V-ing | thay phiên nhau |
| When it comes to… | Khi nói đến… |
| recharge | nạp lại năng lượng |
| I've gotten used to it | Tôi đã quen với nó |
| in a nutshell | nói ngắn gọn |
