# 2. Nhà ở (Home / Apartment)

Chủ đề **gần như chắc chắn xuất hiện** vì Background Survey luôn hỏi nơi bạn sống.

---

## Câu 1 – Mô tả nhà

> **Q:** *"You indicated that you live in an apartment. Describe your home. What does it look like? How many rooms are there?"*

> Sure. I live in an apartment on the twelfth floor of a building in the Cau Giay district. It's not very big, but it's really cozy, and I love it.
>
> When you walk in, the first thing you see is the living room. It has a gray sofa, a small coffee table, and a TV on the wall. There's also a big window, so we get a lot of natural light, and at night you can see the city lights, which is pretty amazing.
>
> Next to the living room is the kitchen. It's quite small, but it has everything we need — a fridge, a stove, and a microwave. Then there are two bedrooms and one bathroom. My bedroom is my favorite place in the apartment. I have a comfortable bed, a desk where I work and study, and a bookshelf full of novels.
>
> The best thing about my apartment is the location. There's a supermarket right downstairs and a park just five minutes away. The only downside is that the elevator is sometimes really crowded in the morning. But overall, I'm really happy living there.

## Câu 2 – Thói quen ở nhà

> **Q:** *"What do you usually do at home on weekdays and on weekends?"*

> Well, my routine on weekdays and weekends is quite different.
>
> On weekdays, I'm usually so busy that I only spend time at home in the evening. After I get home from work at around six, I take a shower first, and then I cook a simple dinner — usually rice, some vegetables, and soup. After dinner, I wash the dishes and watch a couple of episodes of a TV series or scroll through social media. I try to go to bed before eleven, but to be honest, that doesn't always happen.
>
> On weekends, I have a lot more free time. On Saturday mornings, I usually sleep in, and then I do the housework — I vacuum the floor, do the laundry, and water my plants. In the afternoon, I sometimes invite friends over and we cook together or play board games. On Sunday, I just relax, read a book, and get ready for the new week.
>
> So basically, weekdays are about getting things done, and weekends are about relaxing.

## Câu 3 – Kinh nghiệm: thay đổi/sự cố ở nhà

> **Q:** *"Tell me about a time when something unexpected happened at your home, or a change you made to your home."*

> Oh, I remember one time something really unexpected happened. It was about two years ago, during the rainy season.
>
> One night, there was a huge storm. I was sleeping, and suddenly I heard a strange dripping sound. When I turned on the light, I saw that water was leaking from the ceiling in my bedroom! It turned out that the pipe in the apartment upstairs had broken.
>
> I panicked a little at first. I quickly moved my laptop and books away and put a bucket under the leak. Then I went upstairs and knocked on my neighbor's door. Luckily, he was really nice about it. He turned off the water right away and called a plumber the next morning.
>
> It took about a week to fix the ceiling and repaint the wall, and I had to sleep on the sofa during that time, which wasn't very comfortable. But in the end, my neighbor paid for all the repairs, and we actually became good friends after that.
>
> Since then, I always check the ceiling and the pipes before the rainy season. I learned that it's better to be prepared than to be sorry.

## Câu 4 – So sánh nhà cũ và nhà hiện tại

> **Q:** *"Compare the home you lived in when you were a child with the one you live in now."*

> The house I grew up in was very different from my current apartment.
>
> When I was a kid, I lived with my parents and grandparents in a three-story house in my hometown. It was much bigger than my apartment now, and it had a small yard where my grandfather grew vegetables and kept a few chickens. The neighborhood was quiet, and all the kids on our street used to play together every afternoon.
>
> Now, I live in an apartment in the city. It's smaller, but it's more modern and convenient. For example, there's a security guard, an elevator, and a gym in the building. Also, everything I need, like supermarkets and cafés, is within walking distance.
>
> However, I have to say that I miss the sense of community I had back then. In my apartment building, I hardly know my neighbors, but in my hometown, everyone knew each other.
>
> So, if I had to choose, I'd say my current home is more convenient, but my childhood home was warmer and more peaceful.
