# 4. Nghe nhạc (Listening to Music)

---

## Câu 1 – Thể loại nhạc & ca sĩ yêu thích

> **Q:** *"You indicated that you enjoy listening to music. What kind of music do you like? Who are your favorite singers or composers?"*

> I listen to music almost every day, so it's a big part of my life. My taste is pretty wide, but I mostly listen to pop and acoustic music.
>
> I like pop music because it's catchy and upbeat. When I'm feeling down, a good pop song can instantly put me in a better mood. One of my favorite singers is Taylor Swift. I really admire her because she writes her own songs, and her lyrics are like stories about real life. 
>
> As for Vietnamese music, I really like Den Vau. His songs are a mix of rap and ballad, and the lyrics are simple but very meaningful. They often talk about ordinary people's lives, which I can really relate to.
>
> When I want to relax or focus on work, I switch to acoustic or lo-fi music. It's calm and doesn't distract me. So I guess my music choice really depends on my mood.

## Câu 2 – Nghe nhạc khi nào, ở đâu

> **Q:** *"When and where do you usually listen to music? How do you listen to it?"*

> Honestly, I listen to music pretty much all the time.
>
> In the morning, I put on my earphones as soon as I leave the house. My commute takes about thirty minutes by bus, so music makes the trip feel much shorter. At work, I listen to lo-fi music at a low volume when I need to concentrate on something.
>
> In the evening, when I'm cooking or cleaning, I play music on a Bluetooth speaker. It makes the housework a lot less boring. And sometimes before going to bed, I listen to soft piano music to help me fall asleep.
>
> I mostly use Spotify. I like it because it recommends new songs based on what I usually listen to, so I've discovered a lot of great artists that way. I've also made different playlists for different situations — one for working out, one for studying, and one for rainy days.

## Câu 3 – Kinh nghiệm đi xem concert/biểu diễn

> **Q:** *"Tell me about a memorable live music performance or concert you went to."*

> The most memorable concert I've ever been to was a music festival in Hanoi last year.
>
> My friends and I bought tickets about two months in advance, and we were so excited. On the day of the concert, we got there at around four in the afternoon, even though it didn't start until seven, because we wanted to stand near the stage.
>
> Unfortunately, about an hour before the show, it started pouring rain. We were completely soaked, and I was worried that they might cancel the concert. But luckily, the rain stopped just before it started.
>
> When the first singer came on stage, the crowd went wild. Everyone was singing along and waving their phone flashlights. My favorite moment was when the last singer performed a song that I've loved since high school. I sang every single word, and I actually got goosebumps.
>
> By the end of the night, I had almost lost my voice and my feet were killing me, but it was totally worth it. Even now, whenever I hear that song, it brings back memories of that night.

## Câu 4 – Sở thích âm nhạc thay đổi thế nào

> **Q:** *"How has your taste in music changed over time?"*

> My taste in music has changed quite a lot since I was a teenager.
>
> When I was in middle school, I was really into K-pop. I knew all the dance moves of my favorite groups and spent all my pocket money on their albums. Back then, I liked music that was fast, loud, and energetic.
>
> But as I got older, I started to pay more attention to the lyrics. When I went to university, I began listening to more acoustic music and indie artists, because their songs feel more personal and honest.
>
> Also, the way I listen to music has changed. In the past, I used to download MP3 files onto my phone, but now I just stream everything. It's much more convenient, and I can find almost any song in seconds.
>
> I still listen to K-pop sometimes, especially for nostalgia, but these days I prefer music that helps me relax and think.
