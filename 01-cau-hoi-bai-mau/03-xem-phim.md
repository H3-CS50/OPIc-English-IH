# 3. Xem phim (Watching Movies)

---

## Câu 1 – Thể loại phim yêu thích

> **Q:** *"You indicated that you like watching movies. What kind of movies do you like? Why?"*

> I'm a big movie fan, and I watch all kinds of movies, but my favorite genres are thrillers and animated movies.
>
> I love thrillers because they keep me on the edge of my seat. I really enjoy trying to guess what's going to happen next, and when there's a plot twist at the end that I didn't see coming, it's just so satisfying. One of my all-time favorites is *Parasite*. The story is smart, and it also says a lot about the gap between the rich and the poor.
>
> On the other hand, I watch animated movies when I want to relax. Studios like Pixar and Studio Ghibli make movies that are fun but also really touching. For example, *Inside Out* made me laugh and cry at the same time.
>
> I'm not really into horror movies, though. I'm kind of a scaredy-cat, and if I watch one at night, I can't sleep! So yeah, thrillers when I want excitement, and animation when I want something heartwarming.

## Câu 2 – Thói quen xem phim

> **Q:** *"Describe your routine when you go to see a movie. What do you do before and after the movie?"*

> Usually, I go to the movies about twice a month, mostly on Friday nights or weekends.
>
> Before going, I check the reviews online and watch the trailer to decide which movie to see. Then I book the tickets through an app, because it's faster and I can choose good seats. I always pick seats in the middle, a little toward the back — I think that's the best spot for the screen and the sound.
>
> I usually go with my best friend or my sister. We try to get to the theater about fifteen minutes early to buy popcorn and drinks. I always get caramel popcorn — it's a must for me!
>
> After the movie, we usually grab something to eat nearby and talk about the movie — what we liked, what we didn't like, and whether the ending made sense. Sometimes we end up arguing about it for an hour, which is honestly half the fun.

## Câu 3 – Kinh nghiệm xem phim đáng nhớ

> **Q:** *"Tell me about the most memorable movie-watching experience you've had."*

> The most memorable experience I've had was when I went to see *Avengers: Endgame* back in 2019.
>
> It was the opening night, and the tickets were sold out almost immediately. My friends and I were really lucky to get tickets for a late show, at around eleven p.m. When we got to the theater, it was packed with people, and some of them were even wearing superhero costumes. The atmosphere was electric.
>
> The movie was three hours long, but honestly, it didn't feel long at all. Whenever a favorite character showed up, the whole theater cheered and clapped, like we were at a concert. And near the end, there was a really sad scene, and I could hear people crying all around me — including me, to be honest!
>
> We didn't get out until after two in the morning, and we were exhausted, but we stood outside the theater talking about it for another half hour.
>
> I've seen many movies since then, but I've never felt that kind of energy in a theater again. It's a night I'll never forget.

## Câu 4 – So sánh xem ở rạp và xem ở nhà

> **Q:** *"How has the way people watch movies changed compared to the past?"*

> I think the way people watch movies has changed a lot over the last ten years.
>
> In the past, if you wanted to watch a new movie, you basically had to go to the theater, or wait months to buy a DVD or watch it on TV. Going to the movies was a special event.
>
> Nowadays, thanks to streaming services like Netflix, people can watch thousands of movies at home, anytime they want, even on their phones. It's much cheaper and more convenient. Personally, I watch most movies at home now, especially on weekdays.
>
> However, I think the theater experience is still special. The big screen and the sound system make action movies much more exciting. That's why I still go to the theater for big blockbusters.
>
> One downside of streaming, though, is that people don't focus as much. At home, we often check our phones or pause the movie. So, it's more convenient now, but maybe we enjoy movies a little less deeply than before.
